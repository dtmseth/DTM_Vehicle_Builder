## Color Notes

- **White = C** confirmed from price list (VTX609C, IONSMC, 904SLC)
- **Scene lights / floods / work lights are white by default** — don't parse colors from their SKU suffixes (BB = bail mount/black housing, P = passenger, etc.)
- **Warning lightheads** DO encode color in SKU suffix (A/B/R/C/G for single, D/E/J/K for split pairs)
- **Light bars** need bar-level color rules (duo/trio by zone), not individual SKU suffix parsing
